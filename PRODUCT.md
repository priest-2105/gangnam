# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

English-speaking medical tourists evaluating Korean clinic reviews they cannot read in the original language.

## Product Purpose

Gangnam Beauty Guide helps users assess what a translated clinic review actually supports, rather than asking them to trust a translation at face value.

## Positioning

The Review Trust Lens preserves the original source while separating source facts, AI extraction, independent verification, and subjective opinion.

## Operating Context

Users compare clinic and procedure information while planning elective medical treatment in Korea. Provenance, translation quality, price, timing, and verification status are important to their judgment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- This prototype presents one detailed, mocked review.
- The original Korean review is available on demand.
- AI output is represented by mocked structured data; there is no live AI request, backend, or authentication.
- AI-extracted information must not be presented as independently verified.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Gangnam Beauty Guide. The feature is called Review Trust Lens. Its voice is direct, calm, and transparent.

## Evidence on Hand

All review content and statuses are illustrative prototype data and must not be represented as real patient evidence.

## Product Principles

- Preserve provenance when translation mediates trust.
- Distinguish extraction from verification.
- Make uncertainty legible without overwhelming the reader.
- Prefer one decision-useful review experience over a broad directory.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The responsive interface must support keyboard use, clear focus states, semantic labels, and reduced-motion preferences.
